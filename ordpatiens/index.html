Så spelar du

Free cards are the ones nothing lies on top of — plus the face-up waste card.

Tap a free card, then tap the card that translates it. A right pair leaves the table; a wrong one costs you the streak.

Stock deals a new face-up word. Turn it over as often as you like — every deal can be cleared, but each reshuffle costs 30.

Hint with a card selected reveals its translation; with nothing selected it points at a pair.

Corner marks are the word class: en / ett nouns, att verbs, adj, fras.
